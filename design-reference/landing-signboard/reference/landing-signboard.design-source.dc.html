If something goes wrong: one tap, or one shake.

  1. 1Tap SOS on any screen, or shake the phone three times.
  2. 2Five seconds to cancel, in case it was a mistake.
  3. 3The dialler opens with 112. You press call.
  4. 4Your phone texts each emergency contact a live location link and the nearest hospital, even without mobile data.

Pune Explorer helps you reach emergency services faster. It does not replace them, and it never calls or texts without you.
